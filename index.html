NASA FLIGHT MANUAL & ACADEMY

FLIGHT CONTROLS (DESKTOP & LAPTOP)

  • W / S or ↑ / ↓ : Main Engine Throttle (Accelerate / Decelerate)
  • A / D or ← / → : Yaw Left / Yaw Right
  • Q / E : Roll Left / Roll Right
  • R / F : Vertical Strafe (Up / Down)
  • Z / X : Lateral Strafe (Left / Right)
  • SPACEBAR : Emergency RCS Retro-Brake (Cuts velocity)
  • C : Cycle Camera View (3rd Person Chase • 1st Person Cockpit • Cinematic Orbit)
  • T : Toggle Flight Assist (SAS Stabilization vs Pure Newtonian Drift)
  • 1, 2, 3 : Quick trigger [PULSE SCAN], [COLLECT SAMPLE], [TRANSMIT DATA]
  • P or ESC : Pause Simulation

MOBILE & TOUCH CONTROLS

Use the virtual thumbstick on the lower-left corner to steer pitch and yaw. Use the touch buttons along the bottom bar for throttling, scanning, and braking.

SPACECRAFT ENGINEERING RULES

  • Mass vs Launch Vehicle: If spacecraft wet mass exceeds launch vehicle payload limit, launch will abort! Upgrade to a heavier rocket or lighten chassis/instruments.
  • Power Balance: If net power is negative (drain > solar generation), batteries will drain during flight. RTGs provide continuous power even in deep space.
  • Delta-V & Specific Impulse: Higher Isp (like Ion or Nuclear Thermal) provides greater fuel efficiency and velocity change capabilities.
